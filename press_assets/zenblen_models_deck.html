Computer-vision QA · smoothie vending rig

Six nano models judging every cup.

A fixed camera shoots each cup on the blender seat. Purpose-trained YOLO11n models segment the liquid, find unblended chunks, catch spills, mask the wordmark, and check the station is clean. The liquid mask doubles as a fill gauge — on-device CPU, 5.7 MB per model, no cloud round-trip.

Fill level

Geometry, not a seventh model

The camera is bolted and every cup seats in the same gasket, so the seat line moves by a standard deviation of only 9 px across the corpus. That makes pixel positions calibration constants — no scale reference, homography, or fiducial marker needed — and fill level collapses to the height of the mask standard-seg already produces.

STEP 01

Measure

height = bot_y - top_y from the liquid mask's first and last row.

STEP 02

Gate

Score only upright, seated cups: w_max ≥ 290 and bot_y ≥ 560.

STEP 03

Compare

Against a median of 484 px, σ 32.4, measured over 686 seated cups.

STEP 04

Flag

Below 435 px — median − 1.5σ, a 10% shortfall — raise level=low.
